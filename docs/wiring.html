Before first power-up

  • Continuity-test every supply pair for shorts, and check polarity at each connector.
  • Set the 5 V buck to 5.1 V and the BEC to 6.0 V on a meter before the Jetson or servos are attached.
  • All grounds meet at the battery negative, including the adapter GND and Jetson pin 30/34.
  • The adapter's V+ is not connected to the servo bus.
  • Servo switch OFF for the first boot. Then turn it on and run calibrate.py scan, which should find all 12 IDs.
  • Check the F3 shows the pack voltage in f3_tool.py … info and near 0 A with the servos idle.
